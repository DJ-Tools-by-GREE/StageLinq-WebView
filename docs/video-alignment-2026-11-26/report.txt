METHOD AND LIMITS
The first pass searches 19 independent 12-second spectral windows across each track, then refines matched positions using 3-second waveform correlation.
Accepted candidates undergo a second pass of 3-second waveform windows every 2.5 seconds. At least 95% must match; consecutive interior failures reject a candidate.
Constant-offset spread must stay within 75 ms and fitted drift within 60 ms. Reported offsets are best-fit constants; some analog/mastering differences leave residual timing errors.
At least 95% of the audio duration must overlap the available video. Short fades/tails may be uncovered and produce black at video end; those ranges are listed above and in alignment.csv.
Penny Lane FLAC passed; the AIFF copy drifts and stays unmapped. Fat Boys initially appeared constant but failed dense checks around audio 188–206 s.
Different edits were detected in tracks including Shape of You, Blinding Lights and Opalite. Candidate offsets in the report are diagnostic and are never applied for rejected pairs.
These measurements align the video soundtrack with the deck audio. Final NDI/display latency is separate and needs on-site calibration.
The native macOS decoder/NDI output was not run here; AVFoundation compatibility, particularly VP9-in-MP4, needs the target Mac.
alignment.json retains hashes, window matches, confidence metrics, decisions and method thresholds. alignment.csv is the spreadsheet summary. waveform-verified-*.png and waveform-review-*.png show the comparisons.
